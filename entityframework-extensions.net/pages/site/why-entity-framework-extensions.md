## Performance Without Compromise

Entity Framework is designed for developer productivity, but processing thousands or millions of entities through standard operations can quickly become a performance bottleneck.

Entity Framework Extensions uses optimized bulk operations to dramatically reduce execution time, database round trips, and resource usage. Operations that would otherwise require thousands of individual commands can be completed using highly efficient, provider-specific techniques.

You keep the productivity and familiarity of Entity Framework while gaining the performance required for demanding workloads. There is no need to abandon your existing models, rewrite your data-access layer, or maintain complex custom SQL.

The result is **faster applications, shorter processing times, and infrastructure that scales as your data grows**—without sacrificing the development experience your team already knows.
